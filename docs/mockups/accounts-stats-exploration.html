Recommended — 01 · Ledger Hero

It satisfies the single-card mobile requirement with the least new surface: one component that reflows instead of two layouts to maintain, the quietest fit with the existing StatCard language (white card, tinted badges, uppercase labels), and a null-safe stable ledger row.

  • Least code: same card at every breakpoint — only the flex direction changes at lg:.
  • Most consistent: reuses StatCard's card, badge, and label treatments verbatim; no filled surfaces or bands that don't yet exist in this area of the app.
  • Null-safe: “—” placeholders keep the row from jumping when credit accounts appear.
  • Runner-up: 02 · Brand Hero if you want the balance to visually dominate; 03 · Segmented Stack if you want the strongest per-stat structure.
