A — Calculator Keypad Entry

Amount-first paradigm: an in-app numeric keypad replaces the OS keyboard, so the whole create flow never leaves the screen. A segmented type switcher re-colors the entire sheet (red = expense, green = income, blue = transfer) and swaps the minimal field set below the keypad. Transfer mode replaces the category with source → destination pills and an inline fee field.

New direction

B — Desktop Split-Panel Workspace

Built for wide screens: the left column carries the emotional core (type, amount, description, notes); the right column carries structured pickers — account cards with visible balances and category chips. A sticky summary footer does the transfer math live (amount + fee = total out), eliminating the most common transfer mistake. Transfers additionally render twin source/destination lists.

C — Money-Movement Visualization

Transfer modeled spatially: two account cards connected by a rail with the editable amount sitting inside the connector — you literally watch the money move. An optional fee chip plugs into the flow, and a timeline rail carries date and description. A toggle degrades gracefully to a compact card for plain expense/income entry.
