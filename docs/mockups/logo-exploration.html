Money, kept & growing

Twenty-eight marks, each fusing all three layers of the brief — money (coins, banknotes, the dollar sign), a keeper (wallet, safe, ledger, jar), and growth (rising lines, stacks, steps, arrows). Seven stylistic directions, from friendly duotone to premium product icon, in six elegant palettes anchored in green and blue.
