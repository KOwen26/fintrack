Summary & Recommendation

Direction 1 — Card Stack: Best for primary checking/savings accounts. The emerald hero anchors the page; everything scrolls vertically in a natural narrative flow. Horizontal-scroll quick actions feel native on mobile. Stat cards only appear on desktop, keeping mobile lean. Strong choice when the balance is the hero.

Direction 2 — Bottom Nav Sections: Best when the page has multiple distinct concerns (spending breakdown, transaction history, account details). Mobile tabs keep vertical space efficient. On desktop the tabs collapse into visible section headers — all content visible at once. The collapsible transaction list ("Show 4 more") keeps the default view compact.

Direction 3 — Drawer Detail: Best when transactions are the primary concern and account details are secondary. The drawer pattern is familiar from mobile OSes. Categories are collapsible (default open). Desktop upgrades to a permanent sidebar — the same content, no overlay. Cleanest mobile experience since the main view is just balance + transactions.

Recommendation: Use Direction 1 for account overview pages (balance-first narrative). Use Direction 3 for transaction-heavy views where users spend most time scrolling the list. Direction 2 is a middle ground — use when content sections are equally important and you want to avoid vertical scroll fatigue on mobile.
