Canvas + Edge · The Hybrid

My Pick

Direction 04 · Color Edge + Heavy Accent (Softened)

Here's why I'd go with this direction:

  • Familiar structure — white card with border, same layout as current. Minimal migration from the existing Svelte component.
  • Color where it counts — the left accent strip and tinted background give each account a distinct identity without overwhelming the data.
  • Soft edges — the continuous rounded accent strip (no intersecting rectangles) keeps the warm, approachable feel of the Verdant palette.
  • Scales to mobile — works in 1-column on phones (accent strip anchors the card) and 2-column on tablets/desktop.
  • Zero accessibility risk — white text on color is avoided; all text stays on white backgrounds with full contrast.

Second choice: Direction 01 (Color Flood) if you want maximal color — trade off is text contrast and slightly more complex implementation for negative balances and credit card data.
